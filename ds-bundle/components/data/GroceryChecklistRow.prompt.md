Grocery list item with signal-colored check circle and strikethrough on completion.

```jsx
<GroceryChecklistRow name="Blueberries" signal="beneficial" checked={false} onToggle={toggle}/>
<GroceryChecklistRow name="Avocado" signal="beneficial" checked={true}/>
<GroceryChecklistRow name="White bread" signal="avoid" checked={false}/>
```
