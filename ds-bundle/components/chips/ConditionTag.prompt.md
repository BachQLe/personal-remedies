Pill tag identifying a health condition. Color-coded by condition family.

```jsx
<ConditionTag label="Type 2 Diabetes" colorKey="diabetes" />
<ConditionTag label="Hypertension" colorKey="hypertension" size="sm" />
```

Color families: diabetes→forest-100, hypertension→blue-100, cholesterol→yellow-100, kidney→lavender-100, heart→red-100.
