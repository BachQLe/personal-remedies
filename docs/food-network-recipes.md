# Food Network Recipes to Consider Adding

*Prepared July 2026 for Remedi. Two lists of 50: the top healthy recipes (drawn from Food Network's own dietitian-curated healthy collections) and the most popular recipes (drawn from Food Network's "Top 100 Recipes of All Time" / "Top 50 Fan-Favorite" collections and verified popularity reporting). Every entry is a real Food Network recipe with a verified foodnetwork.com URL — none invented. Method note: foodnetwork.com blocks direct page fetches, so verification was done by mining fetched collection pages and targeted search results.*

*Context: our knowledgebase already carries Food Network recipes under food group `l`, scored per condition via `/suggest`. These lists are candidates for expanding that catalog. The healthy list maps cleanly onto the food groups our API scores best (fish, legumes, vegetables, whole grains); the popular list is what users search for — carrying both lets the app meet users where they are and steer them with per-condition tiers.*

## Recommendation for the knowledgebase

- **Add the healthy 50 first** — they map directly onto the food groups our API already scores highest (fish b1, legumes c2, vegetables e, whole grains f, nuts c3) so they'll surface with strong tiers in condition-driven meal plans, including the breakfast/snack/beverage slots where our current `l`-group catalog is thin.
- **From the popular 50, prioritize the ~8 flagged "reasonably healthy"** (Perfect Roast Chicken, Fish Tacos, Baked Feta Pasta, Guacamole ×2, Baked Chicken Wings, Banana Bread, Deviled Eggs) — popularity with defensible scores.
- The remaining popular picks are still worth carrying: users search for them, and showing an honest low tier for a condition ("limit for hypertension") is exactly the product's value proposition — steering, not censoring.
